## About / Bio (verbatim)
Dr. Shilpa S. is an experienced gynecologist with over 10 years of clinical experience in Obstetrics and Gynecology, with a special focus on infertility and reproductive medicine.

She has completed her MS in Obstetrics & Gynaecology from LTMMC & LTMGH, Mumbai, followed by advanced training in Reproductive Medicine from Kiel, Germany, and a Fellowship in Reproductive Medicine at Indira IVF. She is also trained in advanced gynaecological ultrasound, enabling her to provide comprehensive evaluation and individualized fertility care to her patients.

Over the years, Dr. Shilpa has managed a wide spectrum of gynaecological conditions, high-risk pregnancies and obstetric emergencies, and has developed focused expertise in fertility evaluation, IVF treatment planning, reproductive ultrasound and patient counselling.

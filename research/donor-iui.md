### H3: What is Donor IUI?
> Donor IUI is a fertility procedure in which carefully screened donor sperm is placed directly into the uterus during ovulation to increase the chances of fertilization.
>
> This **process bypasses natural barriers and allows sperm to reach the egg more efficiently**, making it a commonly recommended option in early-stage fertility treatment and **IVF in India** pathways.

- Previous unsuccessful attempts with partner sperm

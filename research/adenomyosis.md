### What is Adenomyosis?
Adenomyosis occurs when the **inner lining of the uterus (endometrium) grows into the muscular wall of the uterus**. This can cause the uterus to enlarge and affect its normal function.

At **Wonder IVF**, we evaluate adenomyosis using advanced diagnostic techniques followed by leading **IVF centers in India**, ensuring accurate assessment and personalized care.

### Causes of Adenomyosis?
The exact cause of adenomyosis is not always clear, but it may be associated with:
- Previous uterine surgeries (such as Dilatation and Currettage,C-sections or fibroid removal)
- Chronic inflammation of the uterine lining
- Age-related changes, commonly seen in women above 30–35 years

Understanding these factors helps in planning the right **IVF clinic in India** strategy.

### Symptoms of Adenomyosis
Symptoms can vary, and some women may not experience noticeable signs. Common symptoms include:
- Heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding
- Severe menstrual cramps
- Pelvic pain or pressure
- Enlarged or tender uterus
- Difficulty in conceiving

Early diagnosis can improve outcomes in **IVF and fertility treatment**.

### How is Adenomyosis Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, adenomyosis is diagnosed through:
- Ultrasound (USG) for initial evaluation
- MRI (if required) for detailed imaging
- Clinical assessment of symptoms

Accurate diagnosis helps in creating a treatment plan aligned with advanced **treatment** protocols.

### Impact of Adenomyosis on Fertility
Adenomyosis can affect fertility by:
- Disrupting implantation
- Affecting uterine contractions
- Altering the uterine environment
- Increasing the risk of miscarriage

With proper management, many patients still achieve successful pregnancy through **IVF in India**.

***Balancing Hormones. Restoring Fertility Naturally & Medically***

At **Wonder IVF**, we understand that PCOS is one of the most common causes of female infertility today. **It affects hormonal balance, ovulation, and overall reproductive health**—making conception more challenging.

With the right diagnosis and personalized care, PCOS can be effectively managed. As part of advanced **fertility treatment in India**, we focus on restoring balance and improving your chances of a successful pregnancy.

In May 2026, a global coalition of medical societies officially renamed PCOS to **Polyendocrine Metabolic Ovarian Syndrome (PMOS)**.

### What is PCOS / PMOS?
The old name focused on "ovarian cysts," but those "cysts" are actually completely normal, tiny fluid-filled pockets (follicles) holding immature eggs that didn't get released. **PCOS/PMOS is not an ovarian disease; it is a whole-body hormonal and metabolic imbalance** that heavily impacts ovulation and fertility.

### Causes of PCOS / PMOS?
PCOD can be influenced by multiple factors, including:
- **Hormonal imbalance** (especially androgens)
- Insulin resistance
- Genetic predisposition
- Lifestyle factors such as diet and physical activity
- Stress and metabolic conditions

Understanding these causes helps us design effective treatment within **IVF treatment in India**.

### Symptoms PCOS / PMOS?
Symptoms may vary from person to person, but commonly include:
- Irregular or missed periods
- Delayed or absent ovulation
- Weight gain or difficulty losing weight
- Acne and excessive hair growth
- Difficulty in conceiving

Early identification is important for better outcomes.

### How is PCOS Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, PCOS is diagnosed using a combination of:
- **Hormonal tests** (LH, FSH, AMH, insulin levels)
- **Ultrasound (USG)** to assess ovarian structure
- **Clinical evaluation of symptoms** ovaries

This detailed approach ensures accurate diagnosis aligned with advanced **IVF treatment in India** protocols.

### Treatment Options for PCOS
PCOS is manageable with the right combination of lifestyle changes and medical support.

At **Wonder IVF**, we offer:
- **Lifestyle modification guidance** (diet, exercise, weight management)
- **Medications to regulate hormones and ovulation**
- **Ovulation induction treatments**
- **IUI or IVF** for enhanced conception chances
- **Advanced fertility treatments** in complex cases

Each plan is personalized to suit your body's response.

- Reduces long-term health risks
- Supports overall reproductive health

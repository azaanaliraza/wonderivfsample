## About / Bio (verbatim)
At Wonder IVF, we believe that fertility care is not just about treatment protocols and technology. It is about experience, understanding, trust and having the right doctor beside you through every step of the journey.

We are proud to have Dr. Rinoy Sreedharan as Senior Consultant at Wonder IVF, bringing with him extensive experience in Reproductive Medicine, Assisted Reproduction, Fertility Preservation and Gynaecological Endoscopy.

Dr. Sreedharan holds an MD in Obstetrics & Gynaecology (MICOG) and has undergone advanced training in Reproductive Medicine, Gynaecological Endoscopy and Fertility Preservation. He is also FMF certified for NT scanning.

## Experience & Surgical Expertise (verbatim)
With extensive hands-on experience in assisted reproduction, Dr. Sreedharan has performed more than 5,000 embryo transfer and ovum pickup procedures during his career.

His experience also extends to hysteroscopic and laparoscopic surgery, allowing him to evaluate and treat many of the underlying uterine, tubal and pelvic conditions that can affect fertility.

This combination of reproductive medicine and minimally invasive surgery allows him to look at infertility from a comprehensive perspective — not simply as an IVF procedure, but as a journey where identifying and addressing the right factors can make an important difference.

Over the years, his responsibilities grew to include senior clinical and leadership roles, gaining extensive experience in managing complex fertility cases and large-scale clinical operations. ## Fertility Preservation & Academic Work (eyebrow: "Oncofertility") — verbatim
Dr. Sreedharan has a special interest in fertility preservation and oncofertility — helping patients understand and preserve their reproductive potential when faced with medical conditions or treatments that may affect fertility.

His academic work in this area includes research on oncofertility and pregnancy in adolescent and young adult cancers in India, published in the Journal of Clinical Oncology in association with the American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO). He has also contributed to guidelines relating to breast cancer in young patients and has authored or contributed to several other academic publications.

### H3: What is Egg Freezing?
> Egg freezing, also known as oocyte cryopreservation, is a process where a woman's eggs are collected, frozen, and stored for future use.
>
> Using **advanced vitrification technology, eggs are preserved at extremely low temperatures to maintain their quality**. These eggs can later be thawed, fertilized, and used in IVF when you are ready.
>
> At Wonder IVF, this process is performed with precision, following protocols used by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### H3: How Egg Freezing Works at Wonder IVF
- **Ovarian stimulation** is done to develop multiple eggs
- Eggs are retrieved through a minimally invasive procedure
- Mature eggs are identified and frozen using vitrification
- Eggs are stored in **controlled cryogenic conditions**
- When ready, eggs are thawed, fertilized, and used in IVF

> Each step is carefully managed to ensure safety and high survival rates in **IVF treatment in India**.

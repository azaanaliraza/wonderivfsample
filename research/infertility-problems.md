### What are Infertility Problems?
Infertility refers to the inability to conceive after 12 months of regular, unprotected intercourse (or 6 months for women above 35). It can result from issues in either partner or a combination of factors.

With the support of leading **IVF centers in India**, accurate diagnosis and personalized treatment can significantly improve outcomes.

### Types of Infertility

**Primary Infertility**
When a couple has never been able to conceive.

**Secondary Infertility**
Difficulty in conceiving after a previous pregnancy.

### Common Causes of Infertility Problems
Infertility can arise due to various reasons affecting both partners:

**Female Factors** (bullet list, as rendered — note: rendered with a stray nested bullet "- -")
- Ovulation disorders (PCOS/PMOS)
- Blocked fallopian tubes
- Endometriosis
- Hormonal imbalances
- Age-related decline in fertility
- Premature Ovarian Failure
- Sexual disorders

**Male Factors**
- Low or zero sperm count
- Poor sperm motility or morphology
- Hormonal issues
- Genetic conditions
- Sexual disorders

**Combined or Unexplained Factors**
In some cases, infertility may involve both partners or remain unexplained even after evaluation.

Understanding these causes helps guide effective solutions through **advanced IVF treatment in India**.

### Symptoms of Infertility
Infertility often does not present clear symptoms. However, some indicators may include:
- Irregular menstrual cycles
- Painful periods
- Sexual problems
- Excessive hair growth over face in females.

Early diagnosis plays a crucial role in improving treatment success rates.

### When Should You See a Fertility Specialist?
- If you have been trying to conceive for over a year
- If the woman is above 35 and trying for 6 months
- If there are known reproductive or hormonal issues
- If there is a history of miscarriages

Consulting early at a trusted **fertility clinic** can help avoid delays and improve treatment success.

### How are Infertility Problems Diagnosed?
At Wonder IVF, we follow a comprehensive evaluation process which encompasses:
- Medical history and lifestyle assessment
- Hormonal testing
- Ultrasound and imaging
- Semen analysis
- Advanced diagnostic procedures (if required)

This helps identify the root cause and create a personalized treatment plan.

### Treatment Options for Infertility
Treatment depends on the underlying cause and may include:
- Lifestyle changes and medical management
- Ovulation induction
- IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)
- IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)
- ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)
- Advanced reproductive techniques

These options are part of modern **infertility treatment**, offering improved success rates and better outcomes.

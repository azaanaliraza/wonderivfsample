### Intro / lede
> At **Wonder IVF**, we understand that every fertility journey is different. For some individuals and couples, using donor eggs, sperm, or embryos can open the door to parenthood when other options are limited.
>
> Donor IVF is a safe, ethical, and effective approach that allows you to experience pregnancy and build your family with confidence. As part of advanced **IVF treatment in India**, donor programs are carefully designed to ensure both medical success and emotional comfort.

### H3: What is Donor IVF?
> Donor IVF is a fertility treatment where eggs, sperm, or embryos from a donor are used to achieve pregnancy through IVF.
>
> Depending on your condition, this may include:
- **Egg Donation** – when a donor's eggs are used
- **Sperm Donation** – when donor sperm is used
- **Embryo Donation** – when both egg and sperm are from donors
>
> At **Wonder IVF**, all donor programs follow strict medical, ethical, and legal guidelines followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### H3: Who Can Benefit from Donor IVF?
Intro: "Donor IVF may be recommended in the following situations:"
- Poor egg quality or low ovarian reserve
- Premature ovarian failure or menopause
- Genetic conditions that may be passed to the child
- Severe male infertility or absence of sperm
- Repeated IVF failures
- Single parents or same-sex couples (where applicable)

Closing: "This approach is widely used in modern **IVF in India** to offer new possibilities when conventional treatments are not effective."

### H3: Types of Donor IVF at Wonder IVF
> Each option is carefully discussed and personalized based on your medical and emotional needs.
1. **Egg Donor IVF** — "Healthy donor eggs are fertilized with partner or donor sperm and transferred to the uterus."
2. **Sperm Donor IVF** — "Donor sperm is used when male fertility factors are present."
3. **Embryo Donation** — "Embryos created from donor egg and sperm are transferred for pregnancy."

### H3: How Donor IVF Works at Wonder IVF
- Detailed consultation and evaluation of your fertility profile
- Selection of a suitable donor through regulated and ethical processes
- IVF or ICSI procedure using donor material
- Embryo development under advanced lab conditions
- Embryo transfer in a carefully prepared cycle

> Every step is managed with precision and care, following standards used by trusted **IVF centers in India**.

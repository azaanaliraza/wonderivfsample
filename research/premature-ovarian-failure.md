### What is Premature Ovarian Failure?
Premature Ovarian Failure (also known as Primary Ovarian Insufficiency) **occurs when the ovaries stop functioning normally before the age of 40**. This leads to reduced egg production and irregular or absent ovulation.

At **Wonder IVF**, we evaluate POF using advanced diagnostic protocols followed by leading **IVF centers in India**, ensuring accurate assessment and personalized care.

### Causes of Premature Ovarian Failure?
POF can occur due to various factors, including:
- Genetic conditions or chromosomal abnormalities
- Autoimmune disorders affecting ovarian function
- Previous chemotherapy or radiation treatment
- Hormonal imbalances
- Unknown or idiopathic causes

Understanding the underlying cause helps guide treatment planning within **IVF treatment in India**.

### Symptoms of Premature Ovarian Failure
Symptoms may vary, but commonly include:
- Irregular or missed periods
- Difficulty in conceiving
- Hot flashes and night sweats
- Vaginal dryness
- Mood changes or fatigue

Early diagnosis can improve treatment outcomes in **IVF in India**.

### How is POF Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, we diagnose Premature Ovarian Failure through:
- **AMH Test (Anti-Müllerian Hormone)** – Indicates ovarian reserve
- **FSH and LH Levels** – Elevated levels suggest reduced ovarian function
- **Ultrasound (AFC)** – Assesses follicle count in ovaries
- **Clinical evaluation of symptoms and medical history**

This comprehensive approach ensures accurate diagnosis aligned with advanced **IVF treatment in India** protocols.

### Impact on Fertility
POF affects fertility by:
- Reducing egg quantity and quality
- Causing irregular or absent ovulation
- Lowering chances of natural conception

However, with the right treatment approach, many patients still achieve successful outcomes through **IVF in India**.

### Hormonal Screening in Fertility Care
***Understanding Your Body for Better Fertility Outcomes***

At **Wonder IVF**, we believe that hormones play a key role in fertility and overall reproductive health. Hormonal screening is one of the most important steps in **diagnosing fertility issues and planning the right treatment**.

As part of advanced **IVF treatment in India**, hormonal evaluation helps us understand how your body functions—allowing us to create a precise and personalized treatment approach.

#### What is Hormonal Screening?
Hormonal screening is a series of blood tests that measure different hormone levels involved in reproduction. These **hormones regulate ovulation, egg development, menstrual cycles, and overall fertility**.

At **Wonder IVF**, hormonal screening helps us:
- Assess ovarian function and egg reserve
- **Evaluate ovulation patterns**
- Identify hormonal imbalances affecting fertility
- Guide treatment planning for **IVF** and **IUI**

This is a key diagnostic step followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

#### Why is Hormonal Screening Important?
Even small **hormonal imbalances** can impact your chances of conception.

Hormonal screening helps in:
- Diagnosing irregular or absent ovulation
- Identifying conditions like **PCOS** or thyroid disorders
- Understanding menstrual cycle irregularities
- Planning the right **fertility treatment**
- Improving success rates in **IVF treatment in India**

It provides the clarity needed to move forward with confidence in **IVF in India**.

#### Key Hormones Tested
At **Wonder IVF**, we evaluate a range of important hormones, including:
- **AMH (Anti-Müllerian Hormone)** – Indicates ovarian reserve
- **FSH (Follicle Stimulating Hormone)** – Assesses ovarian function
- **LH (Luteinizing Hormone)** – Helps track ovulation
- **TSH (Thyroid Stimulating Hormone)** – Evaluates thyroid health
- **Prolactin** – Affects ovulation and menstrual cycles
- **Estrogen (E2)** – Supports follicle development
- **Progesterone** – Confirms ovulation and supports pregnancy

Each hormone plays a vital role in determining your fertility health.

#### How Hormonal Screening is Done at Wonder IVF
- A simple blood test is conducted
- Tests are performed on specific days of the menstrual cycle (if required)
- Results are analyzed by fertility specialists
- Findings are explained clearly with next steps

Our approach ensures accuracy and ease, following standards used by trusted **IVF centers in India**.

#### Who Should Consider Hormonal Screening?
Hormonal testing is recommended for:
- Women with irregular or missed periods
- Difficulty in conceiving
- Suspected ovulation issues
- Recurrent IVF failures
- Symptoms of hormonal imbalance
- Planning for IVF or IUI treatment

This is a crucial step in personalized **IVF treatment in India**.

- Supports accurate ovulation tracking
- Reduces trial-and-error treatments

### What is Female Factor Infertility?
Female factor infertility refers to conditions that affect a woman's ability to conceive or sustain a pregnancy. These may involve issues related to ovulation, egg quality, fallopian tubes, uterus, or hormonal balance.

At Wonder IVF, we follow protocols aligned with the best industry standards, ensuring accurate diagnosis and customized treatment plans.

### Causes of Female Infertility
Female infertility may be caused by a combination of medical and lifestyle factors, including:
- Hormonal imbalance
- PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome)
- Thyroid disorders
- Endometriosis
- Age-related fertility decline
- Stress, smoking, and lifestyle habits
- Pelvic infections or surgeries
- Genetic conditions

Understanding these causes allows us to provide effective solutions through **advanced IVF treatment in India**.

### Symptoms of Female Infertility
Many women may not notice symptoms until they try to conceive. However, possible signs include:
- Irregular or missed periods
- Painful menstrual cycles
- Hormonal imbalances (weight gain, acne)
- Pain during intercourse
- Recurrent miscarriages

Early fertility evaluation improves success rates in **IVF in India**.

Treatment depends on the specific diagnosis and may include:
- Ovulation induction and cycle regulation
- Hormonal therapy
- Minimally invasive procedures (laparoscopy/hysteroscopy)
- IVF (In Vitro Fertilization)
- ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)

## About / Bio (verbatim)
For Dr. Kanika Kalyani, fertility treatment has never been only about medicines, injections, embryos or numbers. It is about people, their dreams, their fears, and the hope with which they walk into an IVF clinic.

With over 15 years of experience in Reproductive Medicine and more than 10,000 IVF cycles, Dr. Kanika Kalyani is a highly experienced fertility specialist known for combining clinical excellence, evidence-based medicine and a deeply personal human touch in fertility care.

Having cared for thousands of couples through some of the most emotionally challenging phases of their lives, she believes that every fertility journey deserves to be individualised, transparent and compassionate. She takes time to listen, explain the diagnosis and treatment options in simple language, and involve couples in decisions about their care.

Her philosophy is simple: never treat just a report; treat the person behind the report.

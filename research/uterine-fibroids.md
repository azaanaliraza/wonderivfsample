### What are Uterine Fibroids?
Fibroids are **non-cancerous growths that develop in or around the uterus**. They can vary in size, number, and location, and may or may not cause symptoms.

At **Wonder IVF**, we assess fibroids carefully using advanced diagnostic methods followed by leading **IVF centers in India**, ensuring the right treatment approach for each patient.

### Types of Fibroids
Fibroids are classified based on their location:

**Intramural Fibroids** (H4)
Located within the muscular wall of the uterus

**Submucosal Fibroids** (H4)
Grow inside the uterine cavity and may affect implantation

**Subserosal Fibroids** (H4)
Develop on the outer surface of the uterus

The type and position of fibroids play an important role in fertility and treatment planning.

### Causes of Fibroids
The exact cause of fibroids is not fully known, but they are often associated with:
- Hormonal factors (especially estrogen and progesterone)
- Genetic predisposition
- Age (more common in women in their 30s and 40s)
- Lifestyle and environmental factors

Understanding these factors helps guide treatment decisions in fertility treatment and **IVF**.

### Symptoms of Fibroids
Some women may not experience symptoms, while others may notice:
- Heavy or prolonged menstrual bleeding
- Pelvic pain or pressure
- Frequent urination
- Pain during intercourse
- Difficulty in conceiving

Early evaluation is important for better outcomes in **IVF treatment in India**.

### How are Fibroids Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, fibroids are diagnosed using:
- Ultrasound (USG) for initial detection
- MRI (if required) for detailed assessment
- Hysteroscopy or laparoscopy for further evaluation

This comprehensive approach ensures accurate diagnosis aligned with advanced **IVF** protocols.

### Impact of Fibroids on Fertility
Fibroids can affect fertility depending on their size and location. They may:
- **Distort the uterine cavity**
- **Interfere with embryo implantation**
- **Block fallopian tubes**
- **Affect blood flow to the uterus**

With proper management, many patients achieve successful pregnancy.

### Treatment Options for Fibroids
At Wonder IVF, treatment is tailored based on your condition and fertility goals:
- **Monitoring small fibroids** that do not affect fertility
- **Medications** to manage symptoms
- **Hysteroscopic removal** for fibroids inside the uterine cavity
- **Laparoscopic surgery** for larger or external fibroids
- **IVF treatment** to improve chances of conception

Our approach ensures better outcomes within **IVF in India**.

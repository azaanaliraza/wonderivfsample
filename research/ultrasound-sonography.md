### USG (Ultrasound Sonography) in Fertility Care
***Accurate Imaging for Better Fertility Decisions***

At **Wonder IVF**, Ultrasound Sonography (USG) plays a vital role in understanding reproductive health and guiding fertility treatment. It is one of the **most important diagnostic tools used to monitor ovulation, assess reproductive organs, and support treatment planning**.

As part of advanced **IVF treatment in India**, USG helps provide real-time insights—ensuring that every step in your fertility journey is precise and well-timed.

#### What is USG in Fertility Diagnosis?
USG (Ultrasound Sonography) is a safe, non-invasive imaging technique that uses sound waves to create detailed images of reproductive organs such as the ovaries, uterus, and fallopian tubes.

At **Wonder IVF**, USG is used to:
- **Track ovulation cycles**
- Monitor follicle growth
- Assess uterine health
- Detect abnormalities affecting fertility

This makes it an essential part of diagnosis followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

#### Types of USG Used in Fertility Treatment

**Transvaginal Ultrasound (TVS)**
A detailed internal scan that provides clear images of the ovaries and uterus. It is commonly used for follicle monitoring and early pregnancy assessment.

**Transabdominal Ultrasound**
Performed externally, this scan provides a broader view of pelvic organs and is often used for general evaluation.

Both methods are widely used in modern **IVF in India** to ensure accurate diagnosis and monitoring.

#### Why is USG Important in IVF Treatment?
USG is a key part of fertility treatment as it helps in:
- Monitoring **follicle development** during ovulation
- Determining the best time for **IUI** or **IVF** procedures
- Evaluating uterine lining (endometrium) for implantation
- Detecting cysts, fibroids, or structural abnormalities
- **Tracking early pregnancy development**

Accurate ultrasound monitoring improves the timing and success of **IVF treatment in India**.

#### Is USG Safe?
Yes. Ultrasound is completely safe and does not involve radiation.

At Wonder IVF:

- Safe for both diagnosis and pregnancy monitoring

As part of advanced **IVF in India**, USG is a reliable and essential diagnostic tool.

***The First Step Toward the Right Treatment***

#### Why Fertility Diagnosis Matters
Infertility is not a single condition—it can be influenced by multiple factors related to both male and female health. **Without proper diagnosis, treatments may become repetitive, confusing, and less effective**.

At Wonder IVF, our diagnostic approach helps:
- Identify the exact cause of infertility
- Avoid unnecessary or ineffective treatments
- Improve success rates of IVF and IUI
- Create a personalized treatment roadmap
- Save time, emotional effort, and cost

Accurate diagnosis is what transforms treatment into results.

#### Comprehensive Fertility Evaluation at Wonder IVF
We follow a structured and detailed diagnostic process designed to assess every aspect of reproductive health.

#### Female Fertility Diagnosis
Understanding female fertility requires evaluating ovulation, hormonal balance, and reproductive anatomy.

**Key Tests Include:**

**Hormonal Blood Tests**
Evaluate hormones like AMH, FSH, LH, TSH, and prolactin to assess ovarian function and overall reproductive health.

**Ultrasound & Follicular Monitoring**
Tracks ovarian follicles and ovulation patterns to determine fertility timing.

**Ovarian Reserve Testing**
Measures egg quantity and helps predict response to fertility treatments.

**Hysterosalpingography (HSG)**
Checks whether fallopian tubes are open and functional.

**Pelvic Ultrasound / Sonography**
Detects fibroids, cysts, or uterine abnormalities.

**Laparoscopy / Hysteroscopy (if required)**
Advanced diagnostic procedures for deeper evaluation.

This detailed assessment ensures that your treatment plan within **IVF in India** is accurate and tailored.

#### Male Fertility Diagnosis
Male factors contribute significantly to infertility, and proper evaluation is equally important.

**Key Tests Include:**

**Semen Analysis**
Evaluates sperm count, motility, morphology, and overall quality.

**Sperm DNA Fragmentation Test**
Assesses genetic integrity of sperm.

**Hormonal Evaluation**
Checks testosterone and other hormone levels.

**Ultrasound (if required)**
Detects conditions like varicocele or structural issues.

**Advanced Sperm Selection Techniques**
Recommended when standard parameters are not sufficient.

Our approach ensures that male fertility is addressed with the same precision as **female fertility in IVF treatment in India**.

#### When Should You Seek Fertility Diagnosis?
You should consider a fertility evaluation if:
- You've been trying to conceive for 12 months (or 6 months if over 35)
- You have irregular periods or ovulation issues
- You've experienced recurrent miscarriages
- You have known medical or genetic conditions
- There are concerns related to sperm health

Early diagnosis can significantly improve outcomes.

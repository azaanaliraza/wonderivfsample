### Intro / lede
> At **Wonder IVF**, we believe that a successful pregnancy begins with choosing the right embryo. Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT) is an advanced technique used during IVF to evaluate embryos for genetic and chromosomal health—before they are transferred into the uterus.
>
> This approach allows couples to move forward with greater clarity, reducing uncertainty and improving the chances of a healthy pregnancy.

### H3: What is PGT?
> PGT is a specialized testing method performed on embryos created through IVF. It helps identify genetic or chromosomal abnormalities at an early stage, ensuring that only healthy embryos are selected for transfer.
>
> Through PGT, we can:
- Screen embryos for chromosomal balance
- Detect specific inherited genetic conditions
- Identify structural chromosomal issues
>
> By doing this before pregnancy begins, PGT helps avoid complications later and supports better outcomes.

### H3: Who Can Benefit from PGT?
Intro: "PGT may be recommended for:"
- Couples with repeated IVF failures
- History of recurrent miscarriages
- Women of advanced maternal age (35+)
- Known carriers of genetic disorders
- Families with chromosomal abnormalities
- Couples seeking a more informed **embryo selection process**

### H3: Understanding the Three Types: PGT-A vs PGT-M vs PGT-SR (COMPARISON TABLE — verbatim)

| Test | Full Form | Purpose | Who It's For |
|---|---|---|---|
| PGT-A | Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidy | Screens embryos for chromosomal abnormalities (e.g., Down syndrome, Turner syndrome) | Women above 35, recurrent miscarriages, repeated IVF failures |
| PGT-M | Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic Disorders | Detects single-gene inherited conditions (e.g., Thalassemia, Cystic Fibrosis) | Couples who are known carriers of genetic disorders or have family history |
| PGT-SR | Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Structural Rearrangements | Identifies chromosomal structural issues (e.g., translocations, inversions) | Couples with chromosomal rearrangements or history of unexplained pregnancy loss |

Footnote (verbatim):
> **\*Note:** All three tests involve a **carefully performed biopsy of a few cells from a blastocyst-stage embryo (Day 5 or 6)** and are conducted without affecting the embryo's development when performed under expert supervision.

### Intro / lede
> At **Wonder IVF**, we understand that male infertility can be a significant part of the fertility journey. When sperm is not present in the ejaculate or is extremely low in count, advanced retrieval techniques like **TESA and TESE** offer a reliable solution.
>
> These procedures allow us to directly retrieve sperm from the testes—making biological parenthood possible even in complex cases. As part of advanced **IVF treatment in India**, TESA/TESE plays a crucial role in overcoming severe male factor infertility.

### H3: What are TESA and TESE?
> TESA (Testicular Sperm Aspiration) and TESE (Testicular Sperm Extraction) are minor surgical procedures used to retrieve sperm directly from the testicular tissue.
- **TESA** involves using a fine needle to aspirate sperm from the testes
- **TESE** involves a small surgical biopsy to extract sperm from testicular tissue
>
> Both techniques are commonly used when sperm is not found in semen due to conditions like azoospermia.

### H3: Who Needs TESA / TESE?
Intro: "These procedures are recommended in cases of:"
- Azoospermia (no sperm in ejaculate)
- Obstructive or non-obstructive male infertility
- Previous vasectomy or blockage in reproductive tract
- Very low sperm count unsuitable for conventional IVF
- **Failed sperm retrieval** in semen samples

Closing: "TESA/TESE is often combined with ICSI as part of advanced **IVF in India**, where even a single sperm can be used for fertilization."

### H3: Understanding the Difference: TESA vs TESE? (COMPARISON TABLE — verbatim)

| Procedure | Full Form | Technique | Best Suited For |
|---|---|---|---|
| TESA | Testicular Sperm Aspiration | Needle-based aspiration of sperm from testes | Less invasive cases, obstructive azoospermia |
| TESE | Testicular Sperm Extraction | Minor surgical biopsy of testicular tissue | Severe cases, non-obstructive azoospermia |

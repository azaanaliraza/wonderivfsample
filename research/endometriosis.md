### What is Endometriosis?
Endometriosis is a condition in which tissue similar to the uterine lining (endometrium) grows outside the uterus—**commonly on the ovaries, fallopian tubes, or pelvic lining**.

This tissue responds to hormonal changes, leading to inflammation, pain, and sometimes scar tissue formation, which can affect fertility.

At **Wonder IVF**, we diagnose and manage endometriosis using protocols followed by the **best clinics worldwide**.

### Causes of Endometriosis
The exact cause of endometriosis is not fully known, but it may be linked to:
- Retrograde menstruation (backflow of menstrual blood)
- Genetic predisposition
- Immune system factors
- Chronic Inflammation

Understanding these factors helps guide effective **IVF treatment in India**.

### Symptoms of Endometriosis
Symptoms can vary in severity, and some women may have mild or no symptoms. Common signs include:
- Severe menstrual cramps
- Chronic pelvic pain
- Pain during or after intercourse
- Heavy or irregular periods
- Difficulty in conceiving

Recognizing symptoms early can improve outcomes in **IVF in India**.

### How is Endometriosis Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, we use a combination of methods to diagnose endometriosis:
- **Ultrasound (USG)** for initial evaluation
- **MRI (if required)** for detailed imaging
- **Laparoscopy** for definitive diagnosis and treatment
- **Clinical assessment of symptoms**

This approach ensures accurate diagnosis aligned with advanced **IVF treatment in India** protocols.

### Impact of Endometriosis on Fertility
Endometriosis can affect fertility in several ways:
- Blocking or damaging fallopian tubes
- Affecting egg quality and ovulation
- Causing inflammation in the pelvic environment
- Interfering with embryo implantation

With proper management, many patients achieve successful outcomes through **IVF in India**.

- **IVF treatment** for improved chances of conception
- **Personalized fertility planning** based on your condition

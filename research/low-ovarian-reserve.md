***Understanding Your Fertility Potential, Planning the Right Path***

At **Wonder IVF**, we understand that ovarian reserve plays a crucial role in a woman's fertility. **Low ovarian reserve means a reduced number or quality of eggs in the ovaries**, which can affect the chances of natural conception.

With the right diagnosis and timely care, effective options are available. As part of advanced **IVF treatment in India**, we focus on personalized strategies to help improve outcomes and guide you toward the best possible solution.

### What is a Low Ovarian Reserve?
Ovarian reserve refers to the number of follicles present in the ovaries. An ovarian follicle is a small, fluid-filled sac located inside the ovaries. Each follicle houses a single immature egg (oocyte) and provides the necessary nutrients, hormones, and protective environment for it to grow and mature. A low ovarian reserve indicates that the follicle count is lower than expected for a woman's age, which may impact fertility potential.

At **Wonder IVF**, this condition is carefully evaluated using advanced diagnostic methods followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### Causes of Low Ovarian Reserve
Low ovarian reserve can occur due to several factors, including:
- Increasing age (especially after 35 years)
- Genetic factors
- **Previous ovarian surgeries**
- Medical treatments like chemotherapy or radiation
- Lifestyle and environmental factors

Understanding the cause helps in planning the right **treatment** approach.

### Symptoms of Low Ovarian Reserve
In many cases, there may be no clear symptoms. **However, some signs may include**:
- Irregular or shorter menstrual cycles
- Difficulty in conceiving
- Poor response to fertility treatments
- History of early menopause in the family

Early diagnosis is key to improving outcomes.

### How is Low Ovarian Reserve Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, we use a combination of tests to accurately assess ovarian reserve as required:
- AMH Test (Anti-Müllerian Hormone) – Measures egg reserve
- FSH Levels – Indicates ovarian function
- Ultrasound (AFC – Antral Follicle Count) – Assesses visible follicles in ovaries

These tests help us design a personalized treatment plan aligned with advanced **fertility treatment** protocols.

### Intro / lede
> At **Wonder IVF**, we understand that even the smallest factors can influence the success of an IVF cycle. Laser Assisted Hatching (LAH) is an **advanced technique used to improve the chances of embryo implantation**—especially in cases where the embryo may have difficulty attaching to the uterus.
>
> As part of modern **IVF treatment in India**, LAH is designed to support embryos at a critical stage, helping them implant more effectively and increasing the chances of a successful pregnancy.

### H3: What is Laser Assisted Hatching?
> Laser Assisted Hatching is a **specialized laboratory procedure** in which a precise laser is used to create a small opening in the outer layer of the embryo (zona pellucida).
>
> This process helps the embryo "hatch" more easily and attach to the uterine lining—an essential step for implantation.
>
> At **Wonder IVF**, this technique is performed with advanced equipment and expert care, following protocols used by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### H3: Who Can Benefit from LAH?
Intro: "Laser Assisted Hatching may be recommended in the following situations:"
- Women above 35 years of age
- Repeated IVF failures
- Thick or hardened outer embryo layer
- **Poor embryo development** or quality concerns
- **Frozen embryo transfer** (FET) cycles
- Cases with unexplained implantation failure

Closing: "This technique is carefully advised as part of a personalized **IVF in India** treatment plan."

### H3: How LAH Works at Wonder IVF
- Embryos are developed through IVF in a **controlled lab environment**
- At the appropriate stage, a precise laser is used to thin or create a small opening in the embryo's outer shell
- The embryo is then transferred into the uterus
- The assisted opening helps the **embryo hatch and implant** more effectively

> All procedures are performed with precision, ensuring safety and consistency as per standards followed in advanced **IVF treatment in India**.

### H3: Benefits of Laser Assisted Hatching
- Improves chances of **embryo implantation**
- Supports embryos with a hardened outer layer
- Enhances outcomes in **frozen embryo transfer cycles**
- Helpful in repeated IVF failure cases
- Adds precision to **advanced IVF procedures**

### H3: Is Laser Assisted Hatching Safe? (SAFETY SECTION)
> Yes. When performed by experienced embryologists, LAH is a safe and controlled procedure.

> As part of advanced **IVF in India**, LAH is widely used to enhance implantation success in selected cases.

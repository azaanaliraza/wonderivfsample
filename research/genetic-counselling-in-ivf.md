### H3: What is Genetic Counselling in IVF?
> Genetic counselling is a specialized consultation that evaluates your personal and family medical history to **assess the risk of inherited genetic conditions**.
>
> During this process, you will:
- Understand potential genetic risks
- Learn about available genetic tests like **PGT** (PGT-A, PGT-M, PGT-SR)
- Get expert recommendations tailored to your **fertility journey**
>
> At **Wonder IVF**, genetic counselling is integrated into advanced care protocols followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### H3: Who Should Consider Genetic Counselling?
Intro: "Genetic counselling is recommended for:"
- Couples with a family history of genetic disorders
- Recurrent miscarriages or failed IVF cycles
- Known carriers of inherited conditions
- Women above 35 years of age
- Previous pregnancy affected by genetic abnormalities
- Couples planning advanced procedures like PGT

Closing: "This step is especially important for personalized and effective **IVF in India** treatment planning."

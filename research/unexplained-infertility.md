At **Wonder IVF**, we understand how frustrating it can be when all reports appear normal, yet pregnancy does not happen. This condition is known as unexplained infertility—**where standard tests do not reveal a clear cause, but conception remains challenging**.

### What is Unexplained Infertility?
Unexplained infertility is diagnosed when both partners have normal fertility test results—such as ovulation, sperm quality, and tubal function—but pregnancy does not occur after a reasonable period of trying.

At **Wonder IVF**, we go beyond basic evaluation to explore underlying factors that may not be immediately visible, following protocols used by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### Possible Reasons Behind Unexplained Infertility
Even when tests are normal, certain hidden factors may affect fertility:
- Subtle egg or sperm quality issues
- Minor hormonal imbalances
- Implantation-related challenges
- Immune or inflammatory factors
- Timing-related issues in ovulation or fertilization

Understanding these possibilities helps guide effective treatment within **IVF treatment in India**.

### When is it Diagnosed?
Unexplained infertility is usually considered when:
- A couple has been trying to conceive for 12 months (or 6 months if over 35)
- Ovulation is regular
- Fallopian tubes are open
- Semen analysis is normal

In such cases, further evaluation and advanced treatment planning in **IVF in India** become important.

### Treatment Options for Unexplained Infertility
At Wonder IVF, we focus on improving the chances of conception through a step-by-step and personalized approach:
- **Ovulation induction with timed intercourse**
- **IUI (Intrauterine Insemination)** to enhance fertilization chances
- **IVF treatment** for better control over fertilization and **embryo development**
- **ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)** in selected cases
- **Advanced embryo selection techniques**

Each treatment is carefully planned based on your individual condition within **IVF treatment in India**.

# Privacy Policy

## Legal and Privacy Policy

***Your Trust, Privacy & Safety Are Our Priority***

At **Wonder IVF**, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of privacy, confidentiality, and ethical medical practices. As one of the responsible and patient-focused **IVF centers in India,** we ensure that all personal, medical, and sensitive information shared with us is handled with utmost care and security.

This Legal & Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and manage your information during your journey with us.

### Patient Confidentiality

At Wonder IVF, we strictly maintain patient confidentiality at all times. All personal and medical information shared with us is treated as private and is only accessed by authorized medical professionals involved in your care.

We do not disclose patient information to any third party without your consent, except where required by law or medical necessity as part of **IVF treatment in India**.

### Information We Collect

To provide effective fertility care, we may collect the following types of information:

- Personal details (name, age, contact information)
- Medical history and diagnostic reports
- Treatment-related data
- Payment and billing information data
- Communication records (appointments, queries, follow-ups) data

This information helps us deliver personalized and effective care throughout your IVF in India journey.

### Use of Information

Your information is used to:

- Provide accurate diagnosis and treatment
- Create personalized fertility treatment plans
- Manage appointments and patient communication
- Improve our services and patient experience
- Comply with legal and medical regulations

We ensure that your data is used only for legitimate medical and operational purposes.

### Data Protection & Security

Wonder IVF implements strict security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, misuse, or loss.

We use:

- Secure data storage systems
- Restricted access to sensitive information
- Regular system monitoring and updates

Our practices are aligned with standards followed by leading **IVF centers in India**.

### Consent & Patient Rights

By choosing Wonder IVF, you consent to the collection and use of your information as outlined in this policy.

You have the right to:

- Access your medical records
- Request corrections to your data
- Seek clarity on your treatment and information usage
- Withdraw consent (subject to legal and medical limitations)

We believe in transparency and empower every patient to stay informed during their **IVF treatment in India**.

### Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Information may be shared only when necessary:

- With diagnostic labs or medical partners involved in your treatment
- For legal or regulatory requirements
- With your explicit consent

All third-party interactions are handled with strict confidentiality.

### Website Usage & Cookies

Our website may collect limited non-personal data (such as browser type, pages visited) to improve user experience.

Cookies may be used to:

- Enhance website functionality
- Analyze website performance
- Provide a better browsing experience

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.

### Medical Disclaimer

The information provided on our website is for general awareness and should not be considered a substitute for professional medical advice. Treatment outcomes may vary based on individual conditions, and no guarantees are implied regarding results of **IVF treatment in India**.

### Legal Compliance

Wonder IVF operates in accordance with applicable medical laws, ethical guidelines, and regulatory standards governing fertility treatments in India. All procedures and protocols follow best practices adopted by recognized **IVF centers in India**.

### Updates to Policy

Wonder IVF reserves the right to update this policy as needed to reflect changes in regulations, technology, or services.  Patients are encouraged to review this page periodically for updates.

At **Wonder IVF**, we are committed to protecting your privacy while delivering safe, ethical, and advanced fertility care. Your trust is at the core of everything we do—ensuring your journey through IVF in India is secure, respectful, and confidential.

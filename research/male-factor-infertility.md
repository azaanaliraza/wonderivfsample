### Intro (under H2 "Male Factor Infertility")
***Understanding the Cause. Improving the Outcome.***

Male fertility plays an equally important role in conception. **Male factor infertility contributes to nearly half of all infertility cases**, yet it is often under-discussed.

With the right diagnosis and advanced treatment, many men can overcome fertility challenges. As part of advanced **male fertility treatment**, we focus on identifying the exact issue and offering targeted solutions to improve outcomes.

### What is Male Factor Infertility?
Male factor infertility refers to any condition that affects sperm production, quality, or delivery. It can involve issues with sperm count, motility (movement), morphology (shape), or complete absence of sperm or even sexual problems like Ejaculatory disorders

At **Wonder IVF**, we evaluate male fertility using advanced diagnostic techniques, ensuring accurate assessment and personalized care.

### Common Types of Male Factor Infertility
Rendered as accordion/collapse links (each links to an anchor such as `#collapse-636d0e16abd2998770d5`); full text as displayed:

1. **Azoospermia** — "A condition where no sperm is present in the ejaculate. It may be due to blockage (obstructive) or impaired sperm production (non-obstructive)."
2. **Oligozoospermia** — "Low sperm count, which reduces the chances of fertilization."
3. **Asthenozoospermia** — "Poor sperm motility, meaning sperm cannot swim effectively toward the egg."
4. **Teratozoospermia** — "Abnormal sperm shape, which may affect fertilization."
5. **Oligoasthenoteratozoospermia (OAT)** — "A combination of low count, poor motility, and abnormal morphology—one of the more complex forms of male infertility."

### Causes of Male Infertility
Male infertility can occur due to various factors, including:
- Lifestyle factors (smoking, alcohol, stress)
- Hormonal imbalances
- Genetic conditions
- Varicocele (enlarged veins in the scrotum)
- Infections affecting reproductive organs
- Exposure to toxins or heat
- Previous surgeries or injuries

At Wonder IVF we believe that understanding the specific cause(s) helps guide effective treatment.

### Symptoms of Male Infertility
In many cases, there are no obvious symptoms apart from difficulty in conceiving. However, some signs may include:
- Low libido
- Pain or swelling in the testicular area
- Ejaculation problems
- Reduced facial or body hair (hormonal indication)

Early evaluation improves outcomes.

### How is Male Infertility Diagnosed?
At **Wonder IVF**, we use a comprehensive diagnostic approach:
- **Semen Analysis** – Evaluates multiple parameters like count, motility, and morphology
- **Sperm DNA Fragmentation Test** – Assesses genetic integrity
- **Hormonal Testing** – Checks testosterone and other hormone levels
- **Ultrasound** – Identifies structural issues like varicocele
- **Advanced diagnostic tests** like Karyotype, Yq micro deletion (if required)

This ensures accurate diagnosis aligned with advanced fertility treatment protocols worldwide.

### Treatment Options for Male Factor Infertility
At Wonder IVF, treatment is personalized based on the condition:
- **Lifestyle modification and medical management**
- **Hormonal therapy** (if required)
- **Sperm retrieval techniques (TESA/PESA/MicroTESE)**
- **ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection)** for precise fertilization
- **Advanced sperm selection techniques**
- **Testicular Rejuvenation**

***Clear Insight. Precise Care for Better Outcomes***

#### What is Hysteroscopy?
Hysteroscopy is a procedure in which a thin, lighted instrument (hysteroscope) is inserted through the cervix to examine the inside of the uterus.

Unlike imaging tests, **hysteroscopy provides a direct view—allowing both diagnosis and treatment in the same procedure**.

At **Wonder IVF**, hysteroscopy is performed with precision, following protocols used by leading IVF centers in India.

#### Why is Hysteroscopy Important in Fertility Care?
Hysteroscopy helps identify conditions inside the uterus that may not be visible through routine scans.

It is especially useful for:
- Detecting **uterine abnormalities** affecting implantation
- Evaluating unexplained infertility
- Investigating recurrent **IVF** failures
- Identifying causes of repeated miscarriages

This makes it a key diagnostic and corrective step in advanced **IVF in India**.

#### Conditions Diagnosed and Treated with Hysteroscopy
Hysteroscopy can help detect and treat:
- Uterine polyps
- Fibroids inside the uterine cavity
- Adhesions (scar tissue)
- Septum or structural abnormalities
- **Endometrial irregularities**

By addressing these conditions, hysteroscopy improves the chances of successful implantation during **IVF treatment in India**.
